Our story.

A small team building direct-to-consumer brands for North American and European markets, one small improvement at a time.

  1. Where it began
    A few desks in a co-working space
    We started out in a shared co-working space, where every decision was a bet we couldn't afford to lose. That's where we learned to think before we spend.
  2. How we decide
    Narratives, not slide decks
    We retired PowerPoint. Ideas are now written out in full, read in silence, then debated in the room.
  3. The shake-out
    Still standing when others weren't
    Privacy changes and rising ad costs pushed many brands in our space out. We came through by getting measurably better, month after month.
  4. Today
    A home built for the long run
    Our own office in Petaling Jaya and a team focused on mastering its craft.

How we work.
01
Reliable, every time
When we commit to something, it gets done — not most of the time, but every time. Every action item is written down the moment it’s agreed, and followed through to the end.
02
Every decision is a bet
We list the arguments for and against, weigh them honestly, and size the bet to the evidence. The bigger the bet, the stronger the case has to be.
03
Focus on what moves the needle
Most of our hours go to breakthrough work, finding the next real improvement, rather than busywork. If something doesn't move us forward, we say no to it.
04
AI in every team
AI is built into how every team works. We use it to make each person on the team dramatically more capable. It replaces busywork, not people.

What you're probably wondering.
How does the bonus actually work?

We don't do a once-a-year bonus. Every month, you get paid a percentage on top of your base salary, tied to how the business performed that month. We're profitable, and we believe in sharing that with the people who help make it happen.

Is AI going to take my job?

We're deeply AI-centered — it's built into how every team works, and we train everyone to use it well. But AI doesn't replace people here; it replaces busywork. We're hiring people who can direct AI, not compete with it.

What happens after I apply?

If you're shortlisted, the first interview is online — just make sure you've got a working laptop, webcam, and stable internet. If that goes well, you'll come in for a second interview in person at our office.
